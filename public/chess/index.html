    Wizard's Chess

    A living chess set in a torch-lit hall. Pieces march, leap, shoot and cast and when they capture, the board takes the blow.

    The pieces ♔ King: armored knight with a crown♕ Queen: the witch, who casts lightning♖ Rook: a living stone tower ♗ Bishop: the archer♘ Knight: the leaping barbarian♙ Pawn: a spear-carrying rogue

    How to play

    Click one of your pieces: the squares it can reach light up (blue = move, red = capture). Click a lit square to move. White moves first; the goal is checkmate: trap the enemy King so it can't escape attack.

    ♔ Kingthe crowned KnightOne square in any direction. Never into danger. Must escape when in check (red ring).
    ♕ Queenthe witchAny distance in a straight line or diagonal. The strongest piece.
    ♖ Rookthe stone towerAny distance straight along a row or column.
    ♗ Bishopthe archerAny distance diagonally. It stays on its square colour forever.
    ♘ Knightthe barbarianAn "L": 2 squares one way, 1 sideways. The only piece that jumps over others.
    ♙ Pawnthe rogueForward 1 (or 2 on its first move); captures diagonally forward. On the far side it becomes any piece.

    Special moves: Castling: move the King two squares toward a Rook and the Rook hops over (only if neither has moved and the King isn't in or passing through check). En passant: a pawn that just jumped 2 can be captured as if it had moved 1. Draws: stalemate (no legal move but not in check), 3× repetition, 50 moves without capture or pawn move, or too little material.
