Forest Relic Hunt

Wildwood Colony

Found a colony in an uncharted forest valley. Build roads and production chains, grow settlers into craftsmen and merchants, and lead your Founder into the wilds to recover the five lost relics. Bind them in the Relic Sanctum to win.

Choose your Founder

Your Founder runs the colony and is the hero who explores the wilds and fights for the relics.

Difficulty

How to play

🛤️ Roads & logisticsEvery building needs a road to the Town Hall or a Warehouse (within its range). Carriers fetch the goods. A ⚠️ icon means "no road".
⚒️ Production chainsLumberjack → Logs → Sawmill → Planks. Quarry (next to rocks) → Stone → Stonemason → Bricks. Click a building to see what it needs and why it stops.
🏠 ResidentsHuts fill up when their basic needs are met (Food + a Market in range). Residents pay taxes and work in your buildings. Short of workers? Build more homes.
⬆️ TiersWith enough Settlers and a relic you can upgrade huts to Craftsman houses (they want Bread, Textiles, Ale), then Merchant manors (Jewelry, Tools, Chapel).
⚔️ The FounderPress H to walk out in third person. Explore the fog, open chests, find ore deposits, and clear guarded lairs. Carry relics back to the Town Hall.
🔥 RaidsAfter a while, creatures from nearby lairs raid your outskirts at night. Watchtowers repel them; clear the lair to stop them. Pay to put out fires.
💰 GoldTaxes minus upkeep is shown next to your gold. Keep it positive! The Trading Post lets caravans buy your surplus and sell what you lack.
🏆 VictoryHold all five relics, reach 250 residents and build the Relic Sanctum.
