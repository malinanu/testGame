Wildwood Tactics

The mercenary guilds have turned on each other in the Wildwood. Draft a party of four, outfight rival squads among the rocks and trees, and break the Hollow Crown.

How to play
  • Each hero can move once and take one action per turn, in either order. Then the enemy squad moves.
  • Rock pillars and leafy trees block line of sight. Boulders and trunks next to a target give half cover (−40% dmg, −15% hit).
  • Bushes hide Rogues; corrupted purple trees lash anyone ending a turn beside them; fire burns whoever walks through.
  • Wounds carry between fights. Fallen heroes stay down unless you revive them with a potion at a campfire.
